# Getting Started

### Step 1: Connect Wallet

OddFi supports all major EVM wallets compatible with WalletConnect: MetaMask, OKX Wallet, Bitget Wallet, Phantom, Binance Wallet, Coinbase Wallet, and more.

**Steps:**
1. Click "Connect Wallet" in the top-right
2. Choose your wallet
3. Approve the connection request
4. Sign the authentication message
5. You're in

> First signature auto-registers your account; returning users are recognized by address. On-chain transactions require BSC (chainId = 56) — the platform prompts an automatic network switch when needed.
>
> Mobile: if you have the wallet app installed, it opens directly for authorization; otherwise you'll be redirected to download.

### Step 2: Get ODD

ODD is the sole pricing and settlement token on OddFi. You'll need ODD before placing any bets.

How to get ODD:

| Method | Description |
|---|---|
| Buy on PancakeSwap | Use the ODD/USDT pair on PancakeSwap |
| Cross-chain swap | Use the Relay bridge to swap assets from other EVM chains into ODD |
| Same-chain swap | Swap BNB, USDT, or other BSC tokens into ODD on PancakeSwap |

> ODD is a Clean Token — buying, selling, and transferring are **tax-free**. Referrer rewards come from the bet tax (1%) + payout tax (1pp), both charged at the application layer (see [Referral Rewards](#8-referral-rewards)).

### Step 3: Deposit to Your Account

Go to "Wallet" → "Deposit", copy your personal proxy wallet address, send ODD to that address, and your balance will auto-credit after 12 block confirmations (~36 seconds).

Full details in [Deposit & Withdrawal](#4-deposit--withdrawal).

### Step 4: Start Predicting

Once your balance is credited, browse the matches list, pick your match and outcome, enter the amount, and confirm your bet.
